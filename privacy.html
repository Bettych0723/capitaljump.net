This policy explains what information this website collects, why it is collected, and what you can do about it. It covers www.capitaljump.net only.

Who we are

This website is operated by CAPITAL JUMP INC., Vistra Corporate Services Centre, Ground Floor NPF Building, Beach Road, Apia, Samoa. If you have a question about anything on this page, email capitaljumpinc@gmail.com.

What we collect

Information you give us

If you use the enquiry form on our contact page, we collect the name, email address, company name, service you are interested in, and the message you write. We use it only to reply to your enquiry and to keep a record of the conversation. We do not use it for marketing lists, and we do not sell or share it with third parties.

Information collected automatically

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used — which pages people read, where visitors come from, and which buttons get clicked. This includes:

  • Pages viewed and the order they were viewed in
  • Approximate location (country or city level), derived from your IP address
  • Device type, browser, and screen size
  • How you reached the site (search engine, link from another site, or direct)
  • Clicks on buttons, email links, and enquiry form submissions

When we record that an enquiry form was submitted, we record only that it happened and which service category was selected. The contents of your message are never sent to Google Analytics.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser (named _ga and _ga_<identifier>) so that it can tell returning visits apart from new ones. These are the only cookies this site sets. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and Google’s advertising features (Google Signals) are switched off for this property.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. The site works normally without them.

How long we keep it

  • Analytics data: 14 months, after which Google deletes the detailed records automatically. Aggregated totals may remain in reports.
  • Enquiries: kept for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and any resulting work, then deleted on request.

Who else sees it

Analytics data is processed by Google, which may store and process it outside your country. See Google’s privacy policy for how they handle it. We do not pass your information to anyone else.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email capitaljumpinc@gmail.com and we will reply within 3 to 5 days business day.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
